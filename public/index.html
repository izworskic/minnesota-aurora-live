What this score means

The 0–100 number is our viewing score, not a percent chance. It combines NOAA space-weather signals with local NWS sky cover, darkness, moonlight and Minnesota regional thresholds.

Minnesota Regional Outlook

Northern Minnesota combines high latitude, long northern horizons and extensive dark-sky country. Cloud cover often decides whether a strong space-weather signal becomes visible.

How to use this tonight

Start with the score and headline, check cloud cover and the best dark window, then compare regions before traveling. Voyageurs is the statewide benchmark because of its dark northern horizons and established aurora viewing value.

Data and truth boundaries

NOAA OVATION is a short-term modeled aurora signal and Kp is a broad geomagnetic index. Neither is a local sighting probability. Clouds, twilight, moonlight, light pollution, terrain and horizon quality can prevent a sighting. Missing live values remain missing.
